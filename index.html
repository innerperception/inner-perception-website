About Us

Formed in 2023 by Martin and Georgi as a studio side project, Inner Perception has since evolved into a full live band, bringing a distinctive edge to the post-hardcore genre. Our songs feature shifting dynamics, textured instrumentation, and an underlying emotional pulse, creating a sound that’s both raw and intense but also melodic and emotional.

The band currently consists of Ole (vocals), Martin (guitars,vocals), Honza (guitars), Georgi (bass) and Luca (drums).
